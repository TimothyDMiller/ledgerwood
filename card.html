Ledgerwood on your phone

Your money, made simple: spending, budget, bills, net worth and goals. Free. No account to make.

1. Open your phone's camera and point it at this code.
2. Tap the link that shows.
3. Put it on your Home Screen (below), open it, tap Start.
No camera? Type this into the phone's browser:
timothydmiller.github.io/ledgerwood

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap Share - the square with the arrow.
  2. Slide up, tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Open it from the Home Screen.

Android phone or tablet (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. Open it from the Home screen.

Your money stays on your phone. Later, Ledgerwood on a computer can take it in: on the computer, Use on a Phone. https://timothydmiller.github.io/ledgerwood/
